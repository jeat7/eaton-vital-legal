YOUR DATA · YOUR CHOICE

Delete your Social Circle account

Eaton Vital is a local-first fitness app. Social Circle is an optional online account for sharing selected activities with other users. You can request deletion of this account and its associated cloud data, even if you have already uninstalled the app.

Option 1 — delete inside the app

  1. Open Eaton Vital → Social Circle.
  2. Sign in, if necessary, and open People & privacy.
  3. Expand Delete my Social Circle account.
  4. Type DELETE, choose Permanently delete Social Circle account and confirm.

This option requires you to be signed in to the account you want to delete.

Option 2 — request deletion by email

You do not need the app to request deletion. Email jameseaton7@outlook.com with the subject Eaton Vital Social Circle account deletion, and include the email address used to register for Social Circle.

Email to request account deletion →

For your security: Do not send your password, backup password, full GPS routes, health records or identity documents in the initial request. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing deletion.

What will be deleted?

  • Your Social Circle login and profile associated with the deleted account.
  • Cloud social posts, reactions, comments, messages, memberships and user-linked shared content associated with that account, according to the app's account-deletion process.

What is not deleted automatically?

  • Personal workouts, nutrition entries, saved photos or GPS tracks stored only on your phone.
  • Encrypted Migration Vault archives saved to Downloads or a cloud drive; you control these separately.
  • Copies of information another person has already captured or saved independently.
  • Temporary provider logs or backups retained where required for technical or legal reasons, as described in the privacy policy.

We review verified deletion requests without unnecessary delay and respond in accordance with applicable privacy law. If you have difficulty using the in-app deletion feature, use the email option above.
