PRIVACY AND DATA

Eaton Vital privacy policy

Effective date: 11 October 2026

App: Eaton Vital

Developer / contact: James Eaton · jameseaton7@outlook.com

Eaton Vital is a fitness, nutrition, wellbeing and outdoor activity app. This policy describes information handled by the app, the optional Social Circle feature, external services and your choices. The app uses a local-first design: most personal tracking records are stored on your device, rather than being automatically uploaded to an Eaton Vital account.

1. Information the app may handle

  • Profile and goals: profile name, date of birth, height, weight, goals and preferences if you enter them.
  • Fitness and wellbeing: workouts, routines, sets, activity and step/stair counts, body measurements, recovery information, optional Health Connect data, mood and wellbeing entries.
  • Nutrition: meals, products, nutritional values, goals and food searches.
  • Outdoor activities: precise location while using location-based features, recorded GPS tracks, route plans, maps and photos or notes you add.
  • Social Circle (optional): account email, chosen display name, account identifiers, memberships, invitations, posts, comments, reactions, messages, plans, challenges and other content you choose to share.

2. Information stored on your phone

Personal tracking records such as meals, workouts, steps, photos and original GPS tracks are primarily stored locally on your device. Eaton Vital can request location, physical-activity, camera, notification and compatible health permissions when needed for the features you choose. You can manage or withdraw permissions through Android settings, though some features may stop working.

Local data may be lost if you uninstall the app, clear its storage, lose the device or replace an installation without restoring a backup. The app includes a password-protected Migration Vault export you can save to a location of your choosing.

3. Optional Social Circle cloud services

Social Circle uses Supabase for account authentication, storage and social interactions. When you register or sign in, Supabase processes your account identifiers and authentication information. When you enable sharing, the selected categories and information you choose to post are stored online and become visible according to the app's sharing and circle controls.

Examples of optional sharing include activity summaries, step totals, achievements, nutrition summaries, body-weight changes and wellbeing scores. Named meals and detailed workout sets have separate sharing controls. Original GPS tracks and adventure photos are not automatically uploaded merely because Social Circle is enabled. Other circle members may copy or capture content already shared with them; deleting it later cannot undo independent copies they have made.

Public community profiles and activity (from v4.2.40)

Eaton Vital also offers optional discovery, following and activity sharing beyond private Circles. New adult signups are presented with a clearly explained Public profile option preselected, along with Circle-only and Private alternatives, and must acknowledge public discoverability before registration. Existing Social Circle accounts are not automatically made discoverable: each account owner must activate their community profile first. Public profiles can display their chosen display name, short bio and voluntarily featured fitness highlights to other signed-in Eaton Vital members, including people outside their Circles.

Users can switch visibility to Circle-only or Private, remove their public profile card, unfollow or be unfollowed, and delete public activity posts. Public fitness highlights and voluntary exercise score comparisons contain selected summaries only: the public-community feature does not automatically upload existing meals, complete workout logs, body-weight measurements, health records, raw GPS tracks or photos. Pound-for-pound sharing does not include the person's underlying body weight. Content published publicly may be copied or captured by other people before it is removed. Account-level blocks are enforced in public discovery and public posts as well as private Circles.

4. Online services and third-party requests

Depending on the feature you use, the app may contact third-party providers. These requests can disclose technical information such as your IP address, and location or search terms when needed for location-based services:

  • Open Food Facts for food product searches and barcode data.
  • OpenStreetMap map tiles, Overpass and Protomaps for map content and route discovery.
  • OpenStreetMap/Valhalla routing, Open-Meteo elevation and postcodes.io for optional navigation, terrain and place-search functions.
  • GitHub-hosted Flow Exercise Dataset for some exercise demonstration media when not already available offline.
  • Supabase for optional Social Circle account and sharing functions.

Each external provider operates its own service and may process request data under its own privacy terms. These services may change as features are updated; this policy will be updated if materially needed.

5. Backups and exports

Migration Vault creates an encrypted backup archive protected by a password you set. You choose where to save it, such as Downloads or a cloud drive. Eaton Vital does not automatically upload your Migration Vault file to an Eaton Vital server. If you use OneDrive, Google Drive or another storage provider, that provider handles the file under its own terms. Keep your password secure; forgetting it may prevent recovery.

6. Why the information is used

Information is used to display your health and fitness records, calculate estimates, support personal goals, record and display routes, look up optional reference data, provide features you request and, only when enabled, operate Social Circle. Estimates such as calories burned are informational and are not medical advice. We do not sell users' personal fitness records or use them for targeted advertising.

7. Security

Online requests use HTTPS where available, and Social Circle uses authenticated access and database access controls. Migration Vault uses password-protected encryption. No service or device storage is guaranteed to be completely secure, so protect your phone, account credentials and backup archives.

8. Retention and deletion

Phone records: Local records remain on your device until you remove them, clear app storage or uninstall the app. If you have exported backups elsewhere, you must also manage or delete those copies with the storage provider.

Social Circle: Your cloud account and account-linked content are retained while you use the service. You can request account deletion within Social Circle or through the public deletion page. After identity verification and processing, the account and associated live social records are deleted. Some operational logs or provider-managed backups may persist for limited periods under the applicable provider's retention and legal obligations; they are not intended to remain publicly accessible. We will respond to verified data requests without undue delay and in accordance with applicable law.

Deleting your Social Circle account does not remotely erase separate fitness records, GPS tracks or photographs held only on your phone, or copies that other people previously saved. You can contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information we control.

9. Children and other users

Users should only provide personal information when they are legally permitted to use the relevant features and share information. Parents or guardians can contact the developer about a child's data or an account-deletion request.

10. Updates to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or applicable requirements change. The effective date above identifies the latest published revision.
